Built on the uncompromising Word of God

Redemption Revival Ministry International is a Spirit-filled, Word-based ministry founded on the apostolic mandate to revive souls and transform communities through the power of Jesus Christ.

Located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, we are a church family committed to authentic worship, life-transforming teaching, and community impact — raising a generation that walks in the fullness of God's purpose.

Welcome to Redemption Revival Ministry

"God has called us not merely to church attendance, but to a life of radical transformation. At RRMI, you will encounter the living God — in His Word, in worship, and in community. You are not just welcome here; you are expected."

Pastor Itoro Isaac James is a seasoned apostolic minister of the Gospel with a burning mandate to revive souls and transform communities. He is the Founder and Senior Pastor of Redemption Revival Ministry International, and a passionate teacher of God's Word whose ministry reaches thousands across Port Harcourt and beyond.

Married to his beloved wife, Mrs. God'sgift Itoro, he leads with wisdom, compassion, and uncompromising faith.
