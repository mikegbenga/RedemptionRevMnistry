New Here?

Plan Your Visit

We know visiting a new church can feel like a big step. Here's everything you need to know before Sunday.

Step 1

What to Expect

An hour and a half of Spirit-filled worship, practical teaching from the Word, and a warm welcome from our ushers the moment you arrive.

Step 2

What to Wear

Come as you are. Whether you're in your Sunday best or came straight from a night shift, you're welcome exactly as you are.

Step 3

For Your Children

Children's Church runs during both Sunday services, with age-appropriate teaching and a safe, supervised space for kids to have fun and learn.

Step 4

Getting Here

We're on No. 5 Rumuomoi Market Road, off Psychiatric Hospital Road, Port Harcourt — with on-site parking and greeters to point you the right way.

Who We Are

Built on Faith, Fire
& Purpose

Redemption Revival Ministry International is a Spirit-filled community of believers united under one mandate — to revive souls and transform lives through the living power of Jesus Christ. We are a people of the Word, the Spirit, and the mission.

Founded in Port Harcourt, we carry the fire of God's revival to every street, city, and nation — raising a generation that walks in the fullness of redemption.

A Word from Our Leader

Pastor Itoro Isaac James Founder & Senior Pastor
"

Pastor Itoro Isaac James is a seasoned teacher of the Word of God with an apostolic mandate. He is the Founder and Senior Pastor of Redemption Revival Ministry International in Port Harcourt — a ministry birthed from a burning conviction to revive souls and transform lives through the power of Jesus Christ. He is happily married to Mrs. God'sgift Itoro.

God has not called us to merely manage religion — He has called us to ignite revival. Every soul you see is a candidate for redemption and transformation.
